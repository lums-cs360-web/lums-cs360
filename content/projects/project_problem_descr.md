## **Project 1: Campus Event Discovery and Management Platform**

A centralized platform where students can discover, browse, and RSVP to campus events while event organizers can create and manage their events efficiently.

### **Core Features**
- Event browsing and discovery (talks, sports, clubs, performances)
- RSVP and attendance tracking
- Calendar integration for personal scheduling
- Event creation and management dashboard for organizers
- Ticket management or capacity tracking
- Real-time event updates and notifications
- Event categorization and search filters

### **Primary Users**
- Students  
- Event organizers  
- Student club leaders  
- Administrators  

### **Key Challenges**
- Calendar API integration  
- Real-time notifications  
- Capacity management  
- Event discovery algorithms

## **Project 2: Peer Tutoring Marketplace**

A platform that connects students seeking academic help with peer tutors, enabling scheduling, session management, and quality feedback.

### **Core Features**
- Tutor and student profile creation with subjects/courses
- Tutoring session request and offer system
- Session scheduling and booking
- Rating and review system for tutors
- Session history and progress tracking
- Subject-based tutor matching
- Simple payment integration or credit system (extra)

### **Primary Users**
- Students seeking help  
- Peer tutors  
- Academic advisors  

### **Key Challenges**
- User matching algorithms  
- Scheduling optimization  
- Review system integrity  
- Payment handling (if applicable)

## **Project 3: Campus Sustainability Tracker**

A gamified platform that encourages sustainable behavior by allowing students to log daily activities, track environmental impact, and participate in green challenges.

### **Core Features**
- Daily activity logging (transport mode, energy use, waste)
- Personal impact dashboards with visualizations
- Collective campus-wide impact tracking
- Green challenges and competitions
- Leaderboards and achievement badges
- Sustainability tips and educational content
- Team-based challenges for clubs or departments

### **Primary Users**
- Students  
- Campus sustainability office  
- Environmental clubs  

### **Key Challenges**
- Data visualization  
- Gamification mechanics  
- User engagement strategies  
- Impact calculation accuracy

## **Project 4: Campus Gate Access System**

A security management system that streamlines visitor entry, credential verification, and entry/exit logging for campus gates.

### **Core Features**
- Guard dashboard for visitor credential checking
- Visitor request submission portal for faculty and staff
- Approved entry time verification
- Entry and exit logging system
- Ad-hoc visitor approval workflow
- Guest pass system for students
- Security audit logs and reporting
- Visitor lookup and search functionality

### **Primary Users**
- Security guards  
- Faculty  
- Staff  
- Students  
- Security administrators  

### **Key Challenges**
- Real-time credential verification  
- Approval workflows  
- Audit trail integrity  
- Mobile accessibility for guards  

**Scope Note:**  
Focus on core functionality—guard dashboard, visitor lookup, and entry logging. Advanced features like SMS notifications or facial recognition can be considered for future iterations.

## **Project 5: Counseling Appointment System**

An appointment management platform that connects students with counseling services, enabling easy booking, scheduling, and session management.

### **Core Features**
- Counselor discovery and availability viewing
- Time slot booking system
- Appointment reminders and notifications
- Rescheduling and cancellation functionality
- Counselor availability management dashboard
- No-show tracking and analytics
- Pre-session intake forms
- Post-session feedback mechanism
- Appointment history for students and counselors

### **Primary Users**
- Students  
- Counselors  
- Counseling office administrators  

### **Key Challenges**
- Privacy and confidentiality  
- Scheduling conflict management  
- Reliable notification system  
- No-show prevention  

**Scope Note:**  
Focus on appointment booking flow, availability management, and basic notifications. Intake forms and feedback can be added progressively.
